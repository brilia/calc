The question
f(x) = ax4,  where a is a nonzero constant.
If  f(4)(3) = 48,  find the value of a.
The superscript (4) means the fourth derivative — differentiate four times.
Step 1 — Power rule, first time
f(x) = ax4  ⇒  f′(x) = 4ax3
bring the 4 down, drop the exponent by one: 4x3.
The constant a just rides along — it never changes.
Step 2 — Keep going, four times
f′ 4ax3
f″ 12ax2
f‴ 24ax
f(4) 24a
Each step multiplies the coefficient: 4 → 12 → 24. The a is in every line.
Step 3 — Notice: no x left
f(4)(x) = 24a   — a constant
so  f(4)(3) = 24a   — the "at x = 3" doesn't matter
Once the x's are gone, every input gives the same output. That's why the 3 is a red herring.
Step 4 — Solve for a
24a = 48  ⇒  a = 4824 = 2
Divide both sides by 24 — and check: a = 2 is nonzero, as required.
The answer
a = 2
power rule four times → f(4) = 24a → 24a = 48
