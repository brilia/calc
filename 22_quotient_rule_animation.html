The question
g(x) = 5 − 2x3x + 7,  find  g′(x)
A fraction of two functions — home turf for the quotient rule.
The tool — quotient rule
(low)(d high) − (high)(d low),  over (low)2
ddx[high / low] = (low)·(high′) − (high)·(low′)(low)2
Say the mnemonic out loud — it rhymes with itself, and it sticks.
Step 1 — Name the parts
high  5 − 2x   ⇒   d high  −2
low  3x + 7   ⇒   d low  3
Four ingredients — that's all the rule needs.
Step 2 — Assemble
g′(x) = ()·() − ()·()()2
g′(x) = (3x + 7)(−2) − (5 − 2x)(3)(3x + 7)2
low · d high, minus high · d low — exactly as the mnemonic promised.
Step 3 — Expand & simplify
numerator:  (−2)(3x + 7) − (5 − 2x)(3) = −6x − 14 − 15 + 6x
the x-terms cancel:  −6x − 14 − 15 + 6x = −29
g′(x) = −29(3x + 7)2
The x-terms cancel — a good sign the rule was applied cleanly.
The answer
g′(x) = −29(3x + 7)2
name the parts → assemble → expand & cancel → done
