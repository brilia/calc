The question
x 0 13 12
f(x) π 12 π
f′(x) π 5 π
y = cos−1(f(x))  — find the slope of the tangent at x = 13.
(A) −10√3 (B) −2√3 (C) 23√3 (D) π6
The tool — derivative of arccosine
ddx cos−1(u) = −u′√1 − u2
the minus sign is built into the formula — don't add another one.
Compare with arctangent: there the top was just u′. Here it's −u′.
Step 1 — Read the table at x = 1/3
x 0 13 12
f(x) π 12 π
f′(x) π 5 π
f(13) = 12     f′(13) = 5
The middle column is the one we need — f gives the inside, f′ gives the top.
Step 2 — Apply the formula
dydx = −f′(x)√1 − [f(x)]2
= −5√1 − (12)2
f′ goes on top (with the minus), f goes inside the radical.
Step 3 — Simplify the radical
1 − (12)2 = 1 − 14 = 34   ⇒   √34 = √32
−5√3/2 = −5 · 2√3 = −10√3
Dividing by √3/2 is the same as multiplying by 2/√3.
The answer
(A)  −10√3
(A) −10√3 (B) −2√3 (C) 23√3 (D) π6
(D) is an angle, not a slope — the question asks for the derivative, not the function value.
