The question
g(0) = 3.1  and  f(g(x)) = arctan(1 − x).  Find g′(0).
(A) −32 (B) −14 (C) 14 (D) 32
Step 1 — Differentiate the identity
f(g(x)) = arctan(1 − x)
f′(g(x)) · g′(x) = −11 + (1 − x)2
Chain rule on the left; arctan's derivative (with the inner −1) on the right.
Step 2 — Right side at x = 0
−11 + (1 − 0)2 = −11 + 1 = −12
That's the whole right-hand side — no unknowns in it.
Step 3 — Read f′(3.1) from the graph
g(0) = 3.1 tells you where on f's graph to look.
slope = −3 − 15 − 3 = −42 = −2  ⇒  f′(3.1) = −2
Step 4 — Solve for g′(0)
f′(3.1) · g′(0) = −12
−2 · g′(0) = −12  ⇒  g′(0) = 14
Divide both sides by −2 — the negatives cancel.
The answer
(C)  g′(0) = 14
(A) −32 (B) −14 (C) 14 (D) 32
differentiate the identity → substitute what you know → read the slope off the graph.
